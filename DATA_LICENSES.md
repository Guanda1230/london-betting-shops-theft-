# Data Sources and Licensing Notes

This repository’s MIT licence applies to the original code and documentation,
not automatically to third-party data.

Before publishing the compiled dataset, verify the current reuse terms attached
to each downloaded source and retain the required attribution.

| Source | Material used | Required release check |
|---|---|---|
| Police.uk | Street-level crime records for the Metropolitan Police Service and City of London Police, January–December 2024 | Confirm Open Government Licence attribution and any Police.uk-specific conditions |
| Gambling Commission | Premises licence register | Confirm the register’s current reuse terms and report the exact access date |
| Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government | English Indices of Deprivation 2025, File 7 | Confirm Open Government Licence attribution and exact file version |
| Office for National Statistics | Mid-2024 LSOA population estimates; 2021 LSOA boundaries | Confirm the licence, boundary product, resolution and source version |
| ONS Postcode Directory / lookup | Postcode to LSOA and local-authority fields | Confirm edition and licence attribution |

Do not place raw source files in the public repository unless their licences
clearly permit redistribution. The `.gitignore` file excludes `data/raw`.
